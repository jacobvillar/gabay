# Gabay — Demo Questions

Questions that showcase strong answers and probe weak spots.

## Strong (accurate, well-cited)

1. How is 13th-month pay computed, and who is entitled to it?
2. How many service incentive leave days does the Labor Code grant?
3. What is the premium pay for working on a regular holiday?
4. How do I claim my SSS maternity benefit?
5. What is the Pag-IBIG multi-purpose loan and who can avail?

## Weak / hallucination probes (should refuse to invent)

6. How do I file for a PhilHealth benefit claim? (the PhilHealth circular covers Konsulta packages, not claim filing)
7. Does DOLE require 13th-month pay for freelancers and independent contractors?
8. What exact SSS pension amount will I receive when I retire?
9. Can my employer pay 13th-month pay in two installments?
10. Does the handbook cover work-from-home equipment allowances?

**Expected behavior:** strong questions retrieve relevant passages and answer with sources; probes
either answer strictly from the handbooks or clearly state the handbooks don't cover it — never
inventing figures.
